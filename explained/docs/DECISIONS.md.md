# docs/DECISIONS.md

**What it is:** a log of about 80 design decisions, newest last, grouped by plan step and issue. About 1,500 lines. Each entry says what was chosen, the alternatives, and why.

- **"Chosen by Sean":** you were asked and answered.
- **"Recommended":** made on Claude's recommendation during the autonomous stretches, to be reviewed later.

**When you'd use it:**

- when something looks odd and you want to know if it was deliberate
- before changing an approach: open an issue naming the decision you want to revisit

It's a log, so it's exempt from the 300-line guideline.
