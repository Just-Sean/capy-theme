# src/capy_theme/editor/images_panel.py

**What it is:** `images(theme)` gathers the data for the Components view's images section, per mode:

- every logo slot, saying whether it shows its own image, the light one, or the main logo it falls back to, with its generated sizes
- the generated icons, from the published revision, since icons are made at publish
- the sharing image
- the backgrounds
